# GZ-004 Risks

- Design recommendations may be mistaken for approved product requirements.
- Unknown or asymmetric Requirement/Module/Acceptance/POC/Task references may escape manual review.
- Unexecuted POC assumptions may be written as numeric production commitments.
- Downstream contract tasks may start before GZ-004 is completed.
- A broad requirements baseline can become unreadable unless machine-readable files and a concise human entry point remain synchronized.
